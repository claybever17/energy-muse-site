Meet the founders

Two best friends. One shared vision.

Energy Muse began with two best friends who met at age six.

Heather Askinosie brought her deep study of crystals, energy, and holistic traditions. Timmi Jandro brought her experience in business, product development, and building relationships. Together, they turned one necklace and a shared belief into an enduring wellness brand.

What began with a single intention-based necklace grew into a world of crystals, jewelry, energy tools, frequency experiences, education, best-selling crystal books, and a card deck translated into multiple languages.

More than two decades later, Heather and Timmi are still building Energy Muse side by side—guided by the same question that started it all:

How can we make intentional living more accessible, meaningful, and easy to practice every day?

The work has already been done for you.

Energy Muse searches the world for meaningful products, develops intentional formulas, and considers how every piece can fit into real life.

From wearable combinations and crystals to frequency formulas and energy tools, each element is thoughtfully chosen to become part of a connected practice.

Our methodology.

Everything begins with intention

Every Energy Muse product starts with a purpose. We consider the crystals, shapes, placement, materials, and daily use before bringing each element together.

The result is more than a collection of individual products. It is an ecosystem designed to help you build a practice that feels personal, approachable, and easy to return to.

Formulas with purpose

Our wearable formulas combine carefully selected crystals in numeric patterns based on how they complement one another.

Our frequency formulas take the same thoughtful approach, bringing together frequency, placement, and use. Whether you wear a piece, place it in your space, or bring it into your practice, every detail has a reason for being there.

Treasure hunters at heart

We have always searched for the extraordinary.

From crystals with natural beauty and character to meaningful tools that are difficult to find, every piece is chosen for the story it carries and the role it can play in your practice.

Handpicked and handmade

Natural crystals vary in color, pattern, shape, and texture, making every piece distinct.

Each Energy Muse product is selected and assembled with care using materials chosen for their beauty, quality, and purpose. These natural differences are part of what makes each piece personal.
