About

One necklace became a practice, shared across millions of moments.

Energy Muse began with founders Heather Askinosie and Timmi Jandro, and a simple idea: the objects we choose can help us stay connected to an intention.

Meet the Founders

It started with the need for something real.

Heather’s work with crystals and energy practices, combined with Timmi’s ability to build connection and community, shaped a brand focused on usable guidance rather than inaccessible expertise.

The first necklace wasn’t created to prove a belief. It was created to help carry an intention into everyday life.

The materials evolve.The role remains the same.

Energy Muse continues to explore new forms — copper, geometry, specified frequencies, and digital guidance — because customers deserve more ways to build a practice that fits them.

Every new product still has to answer the same questions: Is it understandable? Is it thoughtfully made? Can someone use it in real life?
