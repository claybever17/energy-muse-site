Getting started.

Do I need to believe in crystals or energy work?

Crystals.

How do I clear or cleanse a crystal?

Smoke, sound, or simply holding it and restating what it is for — all three are safe for every mineral. Avoid blanket advice about water and salt: they will damage some stones. The crystal guide covers which.

Can I put my crystals in water?

Some, not all. Selenite dissolves, malachite and turquoise are damaged by prolonged water, and pyrite can rust. Quartz is generally fine. When you are unsure, don’t — a dry cloth is never the wrong answer.

Will sunlight hurt them?

Prolonged direct sun fades amethyst, rose quartz, citrine and fluorite. A windowsill is the most common way people quietly bleach a stone over a few months.

Does the shape matter?

It changes how a stone is used, not how strong it is. Points direct, clusters radiate, tumbled stones are for carrying, pyramids anchor. Same mineral, different job.

Why doesn’t my stone look like the photograph?

Because it is a natural specimen and no two are identical. Colour, banding and inclusions vary piece to piece. Photographs show a representative example, not the exact item.

Frequency.

What actually is a frequency generator?

A device built around a crystal oscillator and a copper tensor coil, cut for one frequency and matched with a specific sacred geometric shape. It emits that one frequency and keeps emitting it.

Why fixed frequency rather than an adjustable one?

A device that can produce any frequency compromises the circuit for each one, so the signal is weaker and the radius smaller. Ours do one thing, which is why they reach further. The frequency guide explains it in full.

Can I run more than one at once?

Yes. The Formulas are built on exactly that — three frequencies layered in one room, one to clear it, one to steady it, one to aim it.

Does it have to be on all the time?

No. Consistency matters more than duration. Many people run one through the working day or overnight and switch it off otherwise.

How much space does one cover?

One device is designed for one room. For a whole home to feel consistent, that is what the systems are for rather than one device working harder.

Are Energy Muse products medical devices?

No. They’re not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease, and they’re not a substitute for professional medical care.

Jewelry.

What size bracelet should I order?

Measure your wrist snug but not tight and take the size at or just above that number — beads take up room on the inside of the curve. Between two sizes, take the larger. Sizes and bead counts are in the jewelry guide.

Can I shower or swim in it?

Better not to. Water, chlorine, perfume, sunscreen and cleaning products shorten the life of both the elastic and the stone. Put jewelry on last when dressing and take it off first.

My bracelet feels loose. Has it stretched?

Probably, and usually from being pulled over the knuckles. Roll it on and off up the hand instead — that one habit is the difference between a year and five.

What is the little copper piece on the bracelet?

The Energy Muse rondelle — a stamped copper spacer that comes on every bracelet, including the ones you design yourself.
