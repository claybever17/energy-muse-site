How Energy Muse works

Questions, answered plainly.

No belief required, no experience needed. Here is how the products work, how to choose one, and what to expect.

Do I need to believe in crystals or energy work?

No. You can begin with the object, the intention, and the way the practice fits into your life. Energy Muse offers meaning and guidance without asking you to adopt a belief system.

How do I choose a product?

Start with the Energy Quiz or browse by intention. If you already know the format you prefer, shop jewelry, crystals, or frequency tools directly.

What’s the difference between jewelry, crystals, and frequency tools?

Jewelry is a wearable reminder. Crystals are natural objects used in personal practices and spaces. Frequency tools are physical products tuned to specified frequencies and used according to product-specific instructions.

How long before I notice a difference?

Experiences vary. Energy Muse doesn’t promise a specific outcome or timeline. The most useful starting point is a consistent, simple practice and attention to your own experience.

Are Energy Muse products medical devices?

No. They’re not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease, and they’re not a substitute for professional medical care.

How do I care for my product?

Each product page includes material-specific care. We avoid one-size-fits-all cleansing advice, since water, sunlight, chemicals, and abrasion affect materials differently.

Can I use more than one product?

Yes, but more isn’t required. Begin with one primary tool. Add a complementary piece only when it makes the practice clearer or fits a different part of your day.
