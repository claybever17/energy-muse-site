What the shapes mean.

The same mineral is cut different ways for different jobs. The shape is about how a stone is used, not how powerful it is.

Point

Directs. Aim it where you want the intention to go, or toward yourself to draw it in.

Cluster

Radiates in every direction at once. The one for a shared room rather than a personal desk.

Tumbled

Pocket-sized and smooth. Made to be carried and handled, not displayed.

Pyramid

Anchors and amplifies. A stable base with a single point — grounding and focus together.

Sphere & heart

Even in all directions. Softer, steadier, and the usual choice for a gift.

Clearing and care.

We avoid one-size-fits-all cleansing advice, because water, sunlight, salt and chemicals affect minerals very differently — and some of the advice you will find online will damage a stone.

  1. Check the mineral first. Selenite and malachite should not be soaked. Amethyst and rose quartz fade in prolonged direct sun. When in doubt, do not use water.
  2. Smoke, sound or intention are safe for everything. Sage or palo santo, a bell or a bowl, or simply holding it and stating what it is for. None of these can harm a mineral.
  3. Do it on a schedule you will actually keep. Once a month is plenty. A practice you abandon is worth less than a simple one you keep.

Store harder stones apart from softer ones — quartz will scratch calcite and selenite in a shared bowl or a bag.

Where to begin.

One stone, chosen for one reason, kept somewhere you will see it. Add a second only when it does a different job — not because more should work better.
