How to choose one.

  1. Name the thing you want more of. Calm, clarity, protection, abundance, connection. Not the problem — the thing you want instead of it.
  2. Decide where it needs to reach you. If it is a place, place something. If it follows you around, wear something.
  3. Take one, and keep it simple. One object, somewhere you will encounter it daily. That is a practice. Everything beyond it is optional.

What a practice actually looks like.

Less than people expect. You choose the object on purpose, you put it where you will meet it, and when you meet it you remember what it stands for. Some people add a sentence when they put a bracelet on. Some clear a stone once a month. Neither is required.

What matters is that it is small enough that you keep doing it. A ten-minute ritual you abandon in a fortnight is worth less than a two-second one you keep for a year.
